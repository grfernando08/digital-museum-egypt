ABOUT THE PROJECT

Neferti

A digital museum concept designed to make ancient Egypt accessible through interactive technology.

OUR IDEA

History should be explored.

Traditional museums allow visitors to stand in front of objects. A digital museum can allow visitors to travel through them.

Neferti was created around this idea: combining historical storytelling with modern web technology to create an experience that feels less like a textbook and more like a journey.

Every interaction is designed to encourage curiosity, exploration and discovery.

THE DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

Ancient knowledge. Modern technology.

The project uses responsive web design, JavaScript interactions, animations and digital storytelling to create an accessible museum experience.
